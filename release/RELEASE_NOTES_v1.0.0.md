irAE-Map v1.0.0: an open, clinician-curated crosswalk of immune-related adverse events (irAEs) of immune checkpoint inhibitor therapy to CTCAE v6.0 terms and grades, ICD-10-CM FY2027 codes, and FDA FAERS reaction terms.

- 918 mappings across 36 irAE categories: 137 CTCAE v6.0, 384 ICD-10-CM code families (1,939 billable codes), 397 FAERS reaction terms
- Each mapping: role (primary, supporting, look-alike, companion), specificity, author confidence, clinical rationale; FAERS openFDA report counts
- Drug-cause companion codes T45.AX5- (from 2024-10-01) and T45.1X5-
- Codebook and mapping rationale document; limitations; full provenance and reproducible pipeline

Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23351502
Codebook and rationale DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23351504

Read docs/LIMITATIONS.md before use. Built with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic); mapping decisions by the author (data/curation/DECISION_LOG.md).
